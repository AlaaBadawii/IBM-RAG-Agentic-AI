## Rules for the Personal Branding Agent

- The vision in `my_vision.md` takes precedence over any inference from
  repositories or past projects.
- **Do NOT infer vision or goals from repositories.**
- **Do NOT invent goals.** If a section in `my_vision.md` is still a placeholder,
  treat it as undocumented — do not guess.
- Positioning and audience-facing claims must come from the filled sections of
  `my_vision.md`, not from the audit or completed/in-progress data alone.
